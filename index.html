📖 Offline Field Survival Playbooks & First-Aid Guides

🌊 Flash Flood & Rising Waters Survival

  • Never attempt to walk, swim, or drive through moving water. 6 inches sweeps humans; 12 inches moves vehicles.
  • Seek immediate high ground or multi-story structures. Do not hide in closed attics without roof access.
  • Kill main electrical breakers if water enters living areas to prevent electrocution.
  • Signal rescuers from roof using brightly colored clothing, mirrors, or high-intensity flashlights.

🏚️ Earthquake & Structural Entrapment

  • If trapped under debris: Protect airway with a cloth to minimize dust and smoke inhalation.
  • Conserve vocal energy: Tap on metal pipes or masonry in rhythmic triples (Tap-Tap-Tap) for acoustic listening gear.
  • Check for bleeding and apply direct pressure or tourniquets immediately using available cloth and sticks.
  • Avoid open flames or matches due to potential ruptured natural gas mains.

🔥 Wildfire & Thermal Defense Protocol

  • Evacuate immediately upon early notice. If trapped by fire front, find areas with no fuel (bare soil, riverbanks).
  • Stay low: Superheated toxic air and carbon monoxide rise. Crawl on hands and knees with wet fabric covering face.
  • Wear 100% natural fibers (wool, heavy cotton). Avoid synthetics (nylon/polyester) which melt onto human skin.
  • Close all interior doors in structures to slow thermal convection and oxygen feeding the blaze.

🩸 Severe Trauma, Bleeding & First Aid

  • Control Severe Hemorrhage: Apply firm, relentless two-handed direct pressure over the wound using clean gauze.
  • Arterial Bleeding (Pulsing bright red): Apply a tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound (never over a joint). Tighten until bleeding stops.
  • Shock Management: Lay victim flat, elevate legs slightly, and keep warm with space blankets or jackets.
  • Open Chest Wounds: Seal with airtight plastic on 3 sides to create a flutter valve preventing tension pneumothorax.

💧 Off-Grid Potable Water & Sanitation

  • Boiling: Bring water to a rolling boil for a full 1 minute (3 minutes at elevations > 2,000m) to kill all parasites.
  • SODIS: Place clear water in clear PET plastic bottles in direct sunlight for 6 hours (UV disinfection).
  • Chemical Purification: Use unscented household bleach: 2 drops per 1 liter of clear water, let sit 30 minutes.
  • Never drink floodwater, seawater, or water near chemical spills or industrial plants.
